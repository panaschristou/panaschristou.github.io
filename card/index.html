Save it to Apple Wallet

On iOS 27, a compatible iPhone can create a personal pass from the QR image.

  1. Open the card image above on your iPhone and save it to Photos. You can also take a screenshot.
  2. Open the image in Photos and swipe up or tap the Info button.
  3. If Add Pass to Wallet appears, tap it, review the fields, and choose a blue background.
  4. Use Panayiotis Christou for the name and Systems Researcher for the role, then save the pass.

The photo-detection route requires compatible Apple Intelligence hardware and settings. Apple also provides Wallet → + → Create a Pass → Create Pass Manually for a standard pass.

This download is a card image, not a signed .pkpass. Wallet creates and saves your personal pass on your iPhone; its final layout may differ from this image.
