How to create a personal Apple Wallet pass
  1. Save your preferred card image to Photos on your iPhone.
  2. Open it in Photos and swipe up or tap Info.
  3. If Add Pass to Wallet appears, tap it, review the fields and choose your preferred blue background.
  4. Use Panayiotis Christou for the name, Systems Researcher for the role, and PhD Researcher · New York for the additional detail, then save.

Photo detection requires compatible Apple Intelligence hardware and settings. Apple also provides Wallet → + → Create a Pass → Create Pass Manually for a standard pass.
